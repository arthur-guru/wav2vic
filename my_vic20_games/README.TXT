JETR-1.PRG and JETR-2.PRG
=========================
These two files are part1 and part2 of my Bell 206B JetRanger game. 
Part1 loads the graphics over the character set and then prompts for the loading of part2.
Part2 is the plyable game. Who would have thought that 10 years later I would have a 
real private helicopter license - the real JetRanger is much more fun....

Loading multi-part games is difficult on these emulators. The following steps
describe how to load this game for PCVIC version 1.19 (by B.W. van Schooten).
1.  Run the PCVIC emulator.
2.  Press the <Escape> key and select I (Load) then A (Automatic).
3.  Browse to the JETR-1.PRG program and hit the <Enter> key.
4.  Press the <Escape> key and select U (Undelete).
5.  At the VIc-20 prompt type the word RUN then press the <Enter> key.

At this stage the Welcome screen will appear. When the screen displays
the prompt "PRESS PLAY ON TAPE" load the second part of this game as follows:

6.  Press the <Escape> key and select I (Load) then U (Manual).
7.  Browse to the JETR-2.PRG program and hit the <Enter> key.
8.  Press the <Enter> key to take the default start address.
9.  Press the <Enter> key to take the default file type of BIN.
10. Press the <Enter> key to take the default expansion option of U.
11. Press the R key to force a SOFT reset (thanks Mr v-S).

You will now be taken back to the VIC-20 prompt, proceed as follows:

12.  Press the <Escape> key and select U (Undelete).
13.  At the VIc-20 prompt type the word RUN then press the <Enter> key.

Playing.
Your mission is to **DESTROY** - what a brat of a kid I must have been.
Couple that with a leaky fuel tank and dodgy flight controls and if I
can remember I think you have to back out of the hangar in the sky, shoot
the little man that runs past, and when the fuel alarm goes off, go back to
the hangar for a top up without bumping into anything. You get three attempts.

Controls are: Z for left, X for right, / for down, ' for up and <space-bar> for fire.
(Turn on CAPS-LOCK as we didn't have lower case characters in those days).

FROG_1C.PRG
===========
Someone is throwing spears at the frog and he has to jump to avoid being hit.

Load as per normal.


RADAR_1D.PRG
============
From my recollections I think this was a work in progress that never got finished.
the intention was to draw an air-traffic control radar and be able to direct planes
to land at the airport (the center square). No interaction, just watch.
Load as per normal.
